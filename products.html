Need Help Choosing Your Skylight?

Every property is different. Our team can help you explore skylight options based on your roof structure, room layout, and project needs.

Ask Our Team
What types of skylights are available?

Common options include fixed skylights, opening skylights, and tubular skylights. The best choice depends on your daylight requirements, ventilation needs, and roof configuration.

Can skylights help brighten dark rooms?

Yes. Skylights can introduce daylight into rooms that receive limited light through conventional windows. The result depends on the skylight size, placement, roof orientation, and surrounding conditions.

Do skylights provide ventilation?

Opening skylights can support natural ventilation by allowing warm indoor air to escape. Fixed and tubular skylights generally provide daylight rather than an opening for airflow.

Are skylights suitable for every roof?

Suitability depends on roof pitch, structure, roofing material, drainage, and available installation space. A professional assessment can help determine the appropriate solution.

How do I maintain a skylight?

Keep accessible glazing clean and periodically inspect seals, flashing, drainage, and surrounding roof areas for signs of wear. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance guidance.

How do I choose the right skylight?

Consider the room's purpose, desired daylight, ventilation requirements, roof design, glazing performance, and budget. Our team can help you compare suitable options.
